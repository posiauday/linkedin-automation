What this actually is

A content engine configured to how you talk, connected to your LinkedIn through the official API, publishing on the schedule you set.

Not a prompt library. Not a course. It is a system that runs on a server and publishes whether or not you remembered it was Tuesday. You approve what goes out, or you let it run — your call, and you can change your mind later.

It writes from a profile of you: your field, who you're talking to, how blunt you are, and a list of words you would never say. That last part is what keeps it from sounding like everyone else's AI.

How setup goes

  1. You send me your positioning

    What you do, who you sell to, and three posts from anyone whose writing you respect. Fifteen minutes of your time.

  2. I build your profile

    Field, audience, tone, topics, and the banned-words list. This is the part that decides whether the output sounds like you or like LinkedIn.

  3. You read thirty posts

    I generate them, throw out the weak ones, and send you what's left. You mark what's wrong and I retune. Usually one round.

  4. It goes live

    You generate a token from your own LinkedIn account — I never touch your password — and it starts publishing on your schedule.

What it won't do

It won't send connection requests or auto-comment

Those need browser automation, which violates LinkedIn's terms and gets accounts restricted. This uses the official posting API only.

It won't invent numbers about your business

The writing rules forbid fabricated statistics and made-up case studies. A made-up figure challenged in your comments costs more than it earns.

It won't make you go viral

Nobody can sell you that honestly. What it does is remove the reason you stopped posting, which is the actual problem.

It won't replace you in the comments

Replies are where the business comes from. Automating those is how people can tell. That part stays yours.

Questions people ask

How is this different from just using ChatGPT?

It isn't the writing — it's that it runs without you. ChatGPT needs you to show up and prompt it. This publishes on a schedule whether you opened a browser or not.

Will people be able to tell it's AI?

They'd be able to tell if it opened with "In today's fast-paced world." The generator is explicitly blocked from every one of those tells, and you read everything before it goes out.

What does it cost to run after setup?

Roughly $1.30 a month in API fees for daily posts with images, or about twenty cents without them. There's no platform fee — it runs on your own accounts.

What if I want to stop?

Revoke the token and it stops immediately. On Setup and Done-for-you you keep the system either way; there's nothing to cancel.
